tca-api-headless requests

Below the language base of a site with the site set maikschneider/tca-api-headless.
Send Accept: application/ld+json.

GET /_api/headless_pages?path=/some/page     Look up a path: at most one page, its uid
GET /_api/headless_pages/{uid}               A page: SEO fields, meta, regions
GET /_api/headless_navigation/{uid}          A page and its subpages, navigation.depth levels
GET /_api/headless_navigation/{uid}?depth=3  The same with 3 levels, capped at navigation.maxDepth
GET /_api/openapi.json                       The site's OpenAPI document

German: /de/_api/... (the base of the site language)
