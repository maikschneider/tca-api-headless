# Image prompts

Generated with Impeccable 4.11.1 (its image model: gpt-image-2.5-flare), high quality. Every shipped original is a JPEG at quality 80 (mozjpeg), with AVIF/WebP derivatives made by Astro. No text, logos, TYPO3 marks or third-party trademarks were requested. The images have been visually inspected.

Hero is also the default social image. The six feature plates are reused for their social images. Kettenblatt is fictional; its photographs and district map are generated. Originals are in `website/typo3/fileadmin`, reached through FAL and the API; the prompt sidecars are here, no API key.

The three direction comps are not shipped assets; the W2 code-led direction is retained.

The hero and the six feature plates were regenerated in colour for the process-ink palette; their current prompts are under [Colour plates](#colour-plates) and in the sidecars. The first prompts below are kept as the record of the original, cooler set.
